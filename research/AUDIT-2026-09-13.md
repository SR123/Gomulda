# Gomulda webapp audit — 13 September 2026

Scope: fixed colour rules, independent playing budgets, self-play transitions,
saved matches, learning/book compatibility, and affected browser controls.

## Findings and changes

- Violet is now fixed at 25 per square, 50 per pair. Removed both cost inputs
  and provisional wording. The common rules module rejects other sixth-colour
  costs, including in imported books and learning checkpoints.
- Each player has a 0.8, 3, 10 or 30 second move budget. The actual active
  player's budget reaches the worker, for Search, Zero and Search + book.
  The controls also apply to that player's hints. Training controls remain
  separate. Values are saved with the match, rather than one global setting.
- Legacy matches without separate budgets inherit the previous shared level
  for both players. Role swaps and the next watched match retain each budget.
- Fixed a pre-existing series bug: after the first two-round match, the next
  match used default Player 1 White even when Player 1 Black was selected.
  Setup now describes Player 1's first-round role correctly in watched/local
  modes. Programmatic self-play starts through the same validated match path,
  without relying on stale dialog values.
- Legacy custom-cost matches keep their board and submitted colours; scores
  are recalculated under 25. A local backup is attempted before their next
  save. Old custom-cost book/checkpoint data is backed up in IndexedDB before
  falling back to fixed-rule seed data; incompatible imports are rejected.
- Fixed stale arena results: replacing a learning checkpoint clears the
  previous model's visible test table as well as its in-memory arena state.

## Validation

- All 40 Node tests passed, including independent exact-colouring and minimax
  oracles, fixed violet costs, model/book compatibility, budget validation,
  both starting roles, reloads, two full matches, and legacy score migration.
- JavaScript syntax, unique HTML IDs, module paths, local assets and the
  static entrypoint were checked. No whitespace errors in the patch.
- The local browser completed a watched two-round Search-versus-Zero match:
  Player 1 Search at 800 ms, Player 2 Zero at 3,000 ms, starting with Player 1
  Black. Round two correctly made Search White; its reported budget was
  800 ms and observed elapsed time 800.1 ms. Zero's last Black move reported
  3,000 ms budget and elapsed time. Both colour phases completed legally.
- The resulting 2–0 match and both budgets survived reload. This single
  functional run is not a strength benchmark or evidence of optimal play.
- The 390-pixel mobile setup had a 358-pixel dialog with no horizontal page
  overflow. Controls, rules wording and removal of cost inputs were verified.
  The temporary viewport was reset; no browser console errors were observed.

The initial position remains unproved. This audit does not establish playing
strength, a fixed search depth, or hard real-time budget enforcement.
